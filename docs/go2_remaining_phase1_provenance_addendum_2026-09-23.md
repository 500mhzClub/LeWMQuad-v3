The readout distinction matters. `train_go2_dense_visual_motion_readout_development.fit` trains a freshly initialized head on **encoded actual current and future images**. `train_go2_full_heading_readout_development` continues that head. `train_go2_maze_view_readout_development` initializes both matched arms from the same full-heading mixed-data head and uses actual image pairs. These recipes do not train those heads on the historical factorial predictor's generated feature forecasts. At deployment, however, forecast errors from that predictor lineage can affect either head's inputs.

The specifically checked short-pulse, balanced-start, balanced-start-horizon, near-goal and stalled-turn paths retain the narrower conclusions in the preceding report. Near-goal and stalled-turn outcomes are diagnostic **evaluation**, not fitting populations for either current head. The original failed pilot's rendered futures supplied **neither training nor comparative evaluation** to the current predictor/readouts. Its original source decisions and physics have separate evidence. The official pretrained encoder was not fitted on these local branch collections.

The previously documented August waypoint floor-only rendering defect is a distinct, demonstrated caller/schema problem. No training dependency from that waypoint diagnostic to the current native predictor/readout recipes was identified here; this is not a blanket proof about all legacy ancestry. Historical findings and failure records remain unchanged.

If training images in an inherited population were eventually shown to be compromised, a later loss could not be attributed specifically to a representation defect without resolving that confound. Such a finding would **not automatically invalidate a faithful audit of the frozen deployed model using newly qualified source inputs and physical outcomes**. This round establishes neither corruption nor universal historical cleanliness and authorizes no training-data repair.
