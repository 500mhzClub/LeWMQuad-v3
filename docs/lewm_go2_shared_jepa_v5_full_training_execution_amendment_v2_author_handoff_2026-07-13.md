## Exact corrections

### 1. Preflight and exact attempt are separate

V2 defines two one-shot immutable operations:

- payload-free synthetic smoke:
  `.generated/go2_shared_observable_camera_ray_jepa_v5/full_training_v2_preflight`;
- exact attempt:
  `.generated/go2_shared_observable_camera_ray_jepa_v5/full_training_v2`.

The preflight reserves its own namespace before preflight GPU access, uses the
exact production model/loss shapes with deterministic synthetic tensors, opens
no repository payload or learned state, writes an immutable receipt, and
terminates. A different agent must verify and freeze that receipt.

Only a later reviewed execution manifest may bind the receipt. A fresh
standard-library-only operation then reserves `full_training_v2` before the
exact process imports Torch, initializes ROCm/GPU, imports or constructs the
model, opens a V4/V5 checkpoint, opens any RGB/label/role/source payload, or
creates a worker. No live object, GPU context, RNG, tensor, optimizer, cache,
descriptor, or worker may cross from preflight to the exact attempt.

This removes the V1 contradiction: the smoke is explicitly outside the exact
attempt, while the exact reservation remains earlier than every exact neural or
payload open.

### 2. Live readiness status is not authority

The navigation-work readiness Markdown is now path-only informational context.
No hash of its changing bytes may enter source closure, attempt identity,
qualification, or any gate. V2 names the exact V1 plus V2 and their independent
review records as the stable training-design closure. The other frozen V1
parents retain their original authority.

### 3. Selection-role ablation is diagnostic

The no-JEPA arm remains matched and is still evaluated once at the promoted
update on all 495 ordered checkpoint-selection pairs, but the result is
explicitly a reused-development diagnostic. It cannot support a causal or
generalization claim, affect selection/qualification/calibration/G2, promote
the ablation, or trigger an intervention.

Any causal-generalization comparison is deferred to a separately frozen
untouched population and requires a different-agent preregistration PASS before
any contact. Existing G2 is not implicitly available. If a future amendment
authorizes such a comparison, the minimum exact unrounded predicate is:

```text
M_promoted - M_ablation > 0.0
positive family-margin delta in at least 5 of the same 8 families
P_promoted - P_ablation >= 0.0
```

Both admitted-FREE denominators must be positive. `P_promoted >= P_ablation`
is the exact between-arm precision comparator; separate satisfaction of an
absolute precision gate is insufficient.
