# Temporary read-only footprint receipts V1

The completed original controller profile identifies repeated deepcopy of
surface evidence throughout selection, including the ordinary waypoint chain
before anchored recovery. The earlier receipt-sharing experiment only changed
the recovery calculation and improved aggregate paired time by 2.92%. This
separate candidate changes footprint receipt ownership throughout one selector
invocation while preserving the original selection calculation.

A temporary mapper view forwards all bound map methods and attributes to the
original mapper. Its surface view forwards every footprint call, with identical
arguments and no query cache, to the original memory. A returned ordinary
acyclic dict/list graph is copied into read-only dict/list subclasses whose
deepcopy returns the same immutable object. Ordinary mutation methods raise.
Explicit calls to base-class C mutators are outside this internal ownership
contract; the wrapper is not a security boundary. Unsupported graphs, cycles,
custom objects and non-string dictionary keys retain their original behavior.

The complete original anchored selector runs against this temporary view. At
return, ordinary mutable public containers are reconstructed, including tuple
wrappers, with container aliases and cycles preserved. The temporary view and
frozen receipts do not become controller or map state. There is no imported
module mutation, changed model, policy score, threshold, sensor input, retained
memory, feasibility gate, or reduced forecast horizon. The candidate inherits
original observe and advance behavior and adds only two controller metadata
fields. It does not combine earlier copy, batched-query, or recovery-sharing
interventions.

Focused tests cover mutation rejection, public ownership, supported and
unsupported graphs, container aliases/cycles, repeated forwarding, complete
anchored and hold recovery results with both clear and blocked geometry, the
whole selector call boundary, and inherited observation failure behavior.

An exploratory copying workload used authenticated saved original JEPA
selections at observations 3 and 404. Four alternating-order repetitions of
eight ordinary selection copies had medians 0.218260 and 0.241022 seconds;
freezing their surface receipts once, making eight copies, then detaching had
medians 0.038804 and 0.043193 seconds. Complete serialized selection values
matched. This synthetic copy workload is not a controller-latency measurement
or a prediction of the full replay speedup. The recorded tool session was
29723 and exited zero. Fresh-model whole-controller replay remains required.
