# Independent comparison input admission

`scripts/independent_round_trip_population_inputs_development.py` is a library
for the future population launcher. It constructs no scene, loads no model,
starts no process and has no execution entry point. Its full admission requires
the exact original six-case adapter batch to have finished. It does not freeze
the final policy or grant population execution.

Static admission authenticates the fixed source-only eight-layout inventory,
reconstructs it from its source generator, and compares the complete saved
32-case assignment manifest with the original four-arm roster. It binds the
assignment, multiarm integration and population readout verification documents.
Authenticate the completed four-arm adapter factory result, every saved four-
observation stream, its assigned models and controller classes, unchanged three
zero warmups and reactive decisions, actual recorded boundary and evidence scope.
Retain its old failed-cohort input bindings and saved correction admission.
No new-layout sensor packets or native observations are accessed in this stage.

Full admission first requires the fixed six-case batch launch and an explicit
completed result hash. Reconstruct its ordered six-case summary with the
original raw-audit and joint-success checks. Require every original collection
artifact, worker terminal, log, raw audit, startup, readout and parent-completion
receipt, including exact recorded model states and all cross-file relationships.
Require the JEPA and supervised model states to match their preassigned study
arms. The factory's correction admission must equal the batch's original one.
Reexecute the batch's complete original input admission, including training,
correction and adapter evidence verification. No failed or incomplete batch is
restarted or repaired by this helper.

Scientific failures are accepted when the original collection and audit are
complete. Failed strict visibility and zero round trips remain in the six-case
summary and cannot be promoted to success. Missing cases, changed order, models,
controller policy, raw evidence, artifacts, summary or parent receipts reject
admission. Full admission reports input verification only; final_policy_review_
completed and population_execution_permitted remain false.

The future population launcher must still inspect all queued development
outcomes, settle and freeze its final controller/arm protocol before using the
new layouts, authenticate that review and any changed-policy evidence, respect
the original native queue and recheck resources for all remaining cases. The
392-GiB whole-population allowance, fresh per-case controllers/models, matched
startup and complete paired readout remain required. This source addition does
not itself establish navigation, planning or memory advantage, realistic timing,
or bounded real-platform evidence.
