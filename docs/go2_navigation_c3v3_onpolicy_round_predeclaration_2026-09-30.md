# C3-v3 on-policy readout round: pre-declaration, 30 September 2026

**Approval and timing.** Approved by Andrew on 30 September 2026 (the bounded round of the [C3-v2 phase report](go2_navigation_c3v2_phase_report_2026-09-29.md) §7, with the 10-maze safety check). This document is **committed before any maze is registered, any mission is run and any model is fitted.**

**Status of this intervention.**
- **This is the second and final C3 intervention before E1.**
- C3-v1's validation result (13/20) remains the pre-registered capability result.
- C3-v2 remains a recorded model version.
- The harness `v4_completed_support` stays frozen. The V-JEPA 2.1 encoder and the action-conditioned predictor are unchanged. Validation and the sealed set are not used.
- **E1 launches only after Andrew's confirmation, whatever the outcome of this round.**

## 1. Why this round

**Diagnosis on the fresh-check mazes** (phase report, top and §5):
- C3's motion readout decodes only about 0.12–0.21 of true 800-ms forward travel in closed-loop moving states, from actual and predicted future features alike.
- C4 decodes 0.84 and C1 1.01 on the same states.
- The readout lacks coverage of the closed-loop state distribution. Closed-loop forward commands are issued while the robot is already cruising, with sustained forward command history and a command switch 300 ms into the tape. That combination is rare in the offline training and held-out data.

**The change is to training data only.** The data adds closed-loop states driven by C1. Architecture, initialisation, optimiser, number of updates and checkpoint selection are unchanged.

## 3. On-policy data (from C1, not from C3)

**Missions.**
- C1 (command history, unchanged) drives each `onpolicy_fit` and `onpolicy_heldout` episode once, on the frozen V4 harness. Only the episode loader is swapped, as in the fresh check.
- One fresh attempt per episode, with no retries. A failed or timed-out mission still contributes its valid states, and no mission is selected on its outcome.

**Frames.** Frames are regenerated by verified deterministic replay: pass 1 of the capability video renderer, unchanged. Every consumed-packet hash, decision, command, native trace value and mission row must match the log.

**Contexts.** Every camera frame *t* of a mission becomes a context if all of these hold:
1. *t* ≥ 10, and the causal native context (frames *t*−10, *t*−5, *t* and the 1.5-s applied-command history) is complete;
2. the executed applied tape for the eight 100-ms steps after *t* is complete;
3. frames up to *t*+8 exist;
4. the mission has zero disallowed contacts. A mission with any contact contributes nothing.

**Targets.** Physics-true motion in the decision body frame at 100–800 ms (`Recording.targets`). Rows use the C4-v1 and C3-v2 format.

**Known limitation.** The on-policy states are C1's, not C3's. C3's own closed-loop states include more holds and latched turns, so this data covers the cruising and command-switch states the diagnosis identified, not every state C3 visits.

## 4. Fits: a matched pair on identical data

C3-v3 and C4-v3 are trained on the same `train_samples.json`: the C3-v2 training data plus the on-policy fit contexts.

**Batch composition, fixed for both.** Each batch of 64 contexts is 32 "old" + 16 from the C3-v2 maze pool + 16 on-policy.

**C3-v3 motion readout.** Everything else is identical to C3-v1 and C3-v2:
- initialisation `mixed_data_final.pt` (`bbbb05fd…`);
- AdamW (lr 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4), gradient clip 1.0, 440 updates, fixed final checkpoint;
- input: the frozen predictor's pooled prediction for the executed applied tape;
- seed 2026092205, with these draw schedules:
  - the old-context draws and their horizons are exactly those of C3-v1 and C3-v2 (cycle seed +0, horizon seed +10);
  - the maze-pool draws use cycle seed +2 over the 5,216-context C3-v2 pool;
  - the on-policy draws use cycle seed +3 over the on-policy fit pool;
  - the 32 non-old slots take C3-v2's other-horizon schedule (seed +11): slots 1–16 go to the maze pool and 17–32 to on-policy.

**C4-v3 direct predictor.** The C4-v1 plan unchanged:
- architecture of about 17.4M parameters, random initialisation, seed 2026092513;
- AdamW as pre-registered, 1,760 updates, fixed final checkpoint;
- the same batch composition, with a separate queue for each pool.
- The 12-GPU-hour C4 cap counts all previous C4 fits (3.43 h used).

## 5. Offline acceptance for C3-v3 (every criterion must pass)

**How it is scored.** Everything uses the deployed computation (frozen encoder, then frozen predictor with the executed applied tape, then readout). This is the path verified exact on 14,397 fresh-check decisions. Every criterion compares C3-v3 with C3-v2, the current version. Counts are reported for every measure.

**P. Primary: closed-loop moving decisions on the 6 `onpolicy_heldout` missions.**
- **Qualifying decisions:** C1 decision frames with any nonzero applied command in the preceding 1.0 s, whose executed applied tape equals the forward candidate's applied tape and has at least four 100-ms forward steps.
- **P1.** The median predicted/true 800-ms XY translation ratio lies in [0.75, 1.25].
- **P2.** The median 800-ms XY error is at most 50% of C3-v2's on the same decisions.
- **Minimum count.** If fewer than 30 decisions qualify, P cannot be decided and C3-v3 does not pass.
- **Also reported (not gating):** C3-v1, C4-v2, C4-v3 and C1 on the same decisions; the same measures on the 4-step-and-longer forward decisions from rest; and the XY error on all executed decisions.

**R. From rest: the offline held-out windows.**
- **R1.** On the 72 rest-start windows of the existing held-out recordings (§5 group A of the C3-v2 pre-declaration), the median predicted/true 800-ms translation ratio lies in [0.75, 1.25].
- The approximately 6 closed-loop decisions from rest are reported only.

**N. No regression beyond 5%.** Each value for C3-v3 must be at most 1.05 × C3-v2's:
- **N1.** On the existing offline held-out groups (rest-start, in-place turn, other): XY RMSE and yaw RMSE at 500 and 800 ms.
- **N2.** On the 240-window development transfer population: XY RMSE and yaw RMSE at 500 and 700 ms.
- **N3.** In-place turns: the median spurious translation at 800 ms remains at most 10 mm, the C3-v2 threshold.

**C4-v3** is scored with the same measures against C4-v2. This is reported, not gating.

## 6. Closed-loop safety check

This runs **only if C3-v3 passes §5.**
- C3-v3 and C4-v3 each run once on the 10 `safety_check` episodes. These are newly generated mazes, not the fresh-check mazes.
- The harness is the frozen V4, with the frozen readers and the prefix and reader errata.
- Any disallowed contact, hard-clearance violation, technical failure or closeout defect stops new launches and is reported.
- Round-trip counts are reported but do not select.

## 7. Which version enters E1

1. **C3-v3 and C4-v3 enter E1** only if C3-v3 passes every §5 criterion **and** has zero disallowed contacts and zero hard-clearance violations in the §6 safety check.
2. **If C3-v3 passes both but C4-v3 has any disallowed contact or hard violation,** the report flags it and Andrew decides.
3. **Otherwise C3-v2 and C4-v2 enter E1,** under the existing rule (§6 and Amendment 1 of the C3-v2 pre-declaration).
4. **E1's extra training seeds** use the entering pair's recipe and data. Every seed enters, and each seed's acceptance measures are reported (Amendment 1, unchanged).
5. **Nothing launches E1 automatically.** Andrew confirms first, whatever the outcome.

## 8. Budget and stops

- **Running time.** The round's jobs are programme work in the active-wall ledger, estimated at 14–15 h.
- **Calendar stop.** Missions run the frozen owner with its calendar stop unchanged (2 October 03:47 BST). If any mission would launch after 1 October 12:00 BST, the round stops and reports rather than swapping the budget.
- **Storage.** About 3–5 GB, on RecoveryStorage. Nothing is deleted.
- **Stops.** Safety violation, technical failure, or a C4 GPU-cap closeout stops the round, which then reports. Failures are preserved, with no silent retries.
