# Complete tracking result reader and prospective freeze

The outer read-only reader is implemented and tested. It composes the complete
96-stream scoring verifier with exact source/configuration, completed parallel
learning, worker, outside-supervisor and predecessor-result checks. This is
experiment accounting, not a claim that tracking, navigation or deployment
requirements have passed. No native tracking attempt has been launched.

## Reviewed execution and verification boundaries

The reader requires three explicit SHA-256 values: the challenge terminal, the
outside-supervisor terminal, and the prospectively frozen experiment definition.
It accepts only the fixed challenge and supervisor roots. It checks the exact
238-file pre-challenge-terminal roster, cross-bound scoring/comparison hashes,
and absence of a retained failure. It reconstructs the current source/config
definition and invokes the existing complete parallel-learning receipt reader.
Neither fresh-launch preflight nor a simulator initializer is called.

All eight worker requests must match the fixed specification, protocol hash,
launch hash and preceding admitted receipt. Worker exit codes must be integer
zero, and each child receipt must equal the subsequently admitted episode.
The outside terminal must bind the same challenge, definition and request,
record completed child/workload handling, and retain the complete diagnostic
log with exact byte counts and hashes. Recorded parent/keeper identities must
be separate, the keeper outside the workload group, and the saved scope
controls must match the reviewed limits. These checks do not re-observe
historical kernel controls, infer termination from exit zero, or recreate a
collected service. The reader never calls the live-scope admission API.

Only after the complete sensor phase and independent score checks pass does it
reconstruct the six predecessor and eight current witnesses, all 48 comparisons,
and their saved aggregate. It uses the already reviewed narrow predecessor
allowlist. Missing prefixes remain unavailable comparisons, not novel views.
Those witness algorithms and the raw/stress auditors are shared with production;
the separate coverage, pose and continuity arithmetic remains the specifically
independent numerical part. No general scene-independence claim follows.

All declared output sizes and the combined challenge/supervisor envelope are
checked, and source, raw and result byte bindings are checked again at the end.
The new integration avoids the predecessor wrapper's repeated complete RGB
phase reconstructions and one redundant immediate full-cohort hash pass. It
still reauthenticates evidence after inspection. No frozen recorder, learning
source, simulator setting or physical command tape changed.
