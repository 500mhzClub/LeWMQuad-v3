# Sustained-turn native waiter V1

This waiter owns at most one execution of the already tested sustained-turn
maze 02 launcher. It starts no model, raw replay, or native scene while waiting.
It polls the exact original sustained raw replay PID 2813368 and original
extended-budget waiter PID 2793505, including creation time, command line and
boot identity. Their launch hashes are frozen in the source. The budget waiter
represents the entire existing diagnostic queue; the child also verifies that
all original queue owners have ended and reexecutes its full input admission.

Every 30 seconds, the waiter checks the original processes. A live process is
never treated as complete because an observation timed out or an artifact
appeared. After an owner ends, a lightweight check binds its completed result,
launch, status and source closure. Completed identities must remain unchanged
on subsequent polls. Full raw-artifact, model and queue admission occurs in the
child. The maximum prerequisite wait is 48 hours. Expiration or failure is
recorded without restarting, replacing or resuming any process.

After both completed identities are available, the waiter verifies source
bytes, resource capacity, ended original owners and an idle native scene.
It invokes the original prepared launcher once with the exact raw-replay and
budget-waiter result hashes. The child has its own exclusive output directory,
fresh collector and complete raw audit. A nonzero child exit is terminal.

Completion authenticates the entire actual native artifact roster, assigned
model and input definitions, stored worker/collection/audit/readout receipts,
outcome accounting and worker log. It reconstructs the native contact/timing
readout and the complete measured preintervention physical/public prefix.
Scientific failure remains valid completed evidence; a round trip is not
required to finish the waiter. No independent-study policy selection,
navigation qualification, real-time qualification or hardware claim follows.

The source and preparation documents are frozen before waiter execution.
Existing waiter or child output, including symlinks, prevents a second start.
