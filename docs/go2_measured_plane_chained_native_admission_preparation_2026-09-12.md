# Source preparation: admission for a future chained-tracking native pilot

The completed three-arm native pilot comparison is recorded in
`go2_measured_plane_three_arm_native_comparison_2026-09-12.md`. The full-history
timing replay still runs, followed by the already-queued chained controller
replay. No chained native attempt has been launched or frozen.

The new `scripts/measured_plane_chained_native_inputs_development.py` prepares
the input-authentication step for that later decision. It creates no output,
model, replay, child process, or simulator scene and does not select a boundary
or command in advance.

## Admission behavior

- Bind the exact existing chained and reactive waiter launches, including their
  boot, process identities, original queue predecessors, and source closures.
- Reject either live waiter before expensive completed-output reconstruction.
  Reject failure markers, including dangling failure symlinks, without retry
  or queue bypass.
- Require the full waiter artifact rosters, exact result statuses and source
  bindings, unchanged scope flags, and matching saved completion receipts.
- Tie the chained replay to the exact completed learned native result, and
  require the already-completed nominal/reactive comparison identities.
- Reuse `await_go2_measured_plane_chained_controller_prefix_v1.completed_child`
  to reauthenticate closed artifacts and reconstruct consumed public packets,
  complete comparisons, and the saved report. It does not rerun inference.
- Derive the intervention from the actual completed report using the existing
  native-prefix boundary checker. A failed candidate, contaminated report,
  unchanged request/terminal pair, or changed replay identity cannot qualify.
  An admitted terminal-only intervention is distinguished from a command
  change.
- Reuse `await_go2_reactive_measured_plane_native_v1.completed_child` to verify
  the closed native comparison, complete artifact roster, physical prefix,
  readout, and predecessor identities. Negative scientific outcomes remain
  admissible; no success-based selection is introduced.
- Reauthenticate source and waiter artifacts after reconstruction. The returned
  receipt explicitly says that no future native execution, future physical
  prefix, hardware-resource admission, or native serialization check has yet
  occurred. It preserves the actual predecessor prefix flag rather than
  assuming every completed run reached its intervention.

The future launcher must still freeze its protocol and source closure, verify
current resources and native serialization, execute the actual intervention,
and audit its entire collection. These preparations do not establish tracking
recovery, a round trip, real-time performance, or hardware qualification.
