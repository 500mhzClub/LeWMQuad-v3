Implemented a read-only integration of the existing independent coverage, pose
error and continuity checkers across the fixed eight base and 88 stress streams.
This removes the gap between separately tested arithmetic helpers and the saved
complete scoring population. It does not yet finish the complete challenge
reader, and no native experiment or learning fit was launched by this work.

The component authenticates the exact production preterminal artifact roster,
checks matching collection/base/stress phase bindings, then admits the entire
sensor phase before accessing native coordinates. It independently checks all
96 continuity histories, reexecutes the existing raw auditor once per trial,
and independently reconstructs coverage and every saved pose-error stream.
Per-trial and overall score copies, all three aggregate claims, and final
artifact hashes must agree. Physical stops and unavailable poses are retained.
Stress reports retain whether onset occurred, whether the requested tape was
complete, changed-packet counts and per-arm actual intervention exposure.
Unexercised stress is not converted into a successful robustness result.
