## Acceptance and entry into E1

**C3-v3 did not pass the pre-declared offline acceptance** (`c3v3_acceptance_v1/result.json`, `5ce39164…`).
- **Passed:** P1, P2, R1 and N3.
- **Failed:** N1 (no regression beyond 5% on the offline held-out groups) and N2 (the same on the transfer population).

**Under §7 of the pre-declaration:**
- the closed-loop safety check does not run;
- **C3-v3 and C4-v3 do not enter E1;**
- **C3-v2 and C4-v2 enter E1,** under the existing rule.

C3-v3 and C4-v3 remain recorded model versions, with the results in the [round report](go2_navigation_c3v3_round_report_2026-09-30.md).
