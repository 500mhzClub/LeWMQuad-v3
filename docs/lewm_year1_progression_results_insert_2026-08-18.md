# Year 1 progression insert

## Work completed

The work isolated predictive representation, action specificity, rollout
depth, proprioception, spatial retention, utility extraction, and place-memory
compatibility in separate registered assays. The main factorial result used
eight paired seeds and deterministic counterfactual branches.

## Principal result

Two-step autoregressive supervision improved direct future-latent fidelity at
H1–H4 and selected action-specific retrieval outcomes. The H2 equal-family
cosine effect was 0.00808 [0.00643, 0.00972].

## Negative and null results

Proprioception did not materially amplify rollout supervision. Four-step
training improved H4 fidelity but regressed H1 and did not establish more
action differentiation. Occupancy was inconclusive. The fixed-pooling utility
scorer failed qualification; ViT-g showed no scaling signal; memory interfaces
failed their true-target gates; and the place head remains unevaluated because
its panel was unavailable.

## Methodological contribution

The project establishes a reproducible separation between fidelity, action
specificity, place retrieval, and utility scoring, with equal-family primary
estimators and explicit technical-validity custody.

## Implications for Year 2

Future work should evaluate a dedicated place representation independently of
predictive dynamics, using the `EVALUATION_FIRST_SINGLE_SEED` rule before any
replication.

## Revised research risks and work packages

Risks are now concentrated in place-space construction, graph coverage,
environment generalization, and the fidelity-to-planning gap. No new model is
authorized by this insert.
