request must match its actual prospective decision, and the recorded physical

All eleven original physics fields must be present with consistent bounded
populations. Every field's complete preintervention data is fingerprinted;

For every common observation, the checker reconstructs both public packets
with the appropriate original/extended reader and compares their full hashes
and clocks to the saved prospective replay. The old native decision must match
its original saved hash, the new native decision must exactly match the
prospective candidate, and the saved baseline must reproduce the old native
decision through the existing performance normalization. The complete budget
comparison is reconstructed again; no earlier intervention is permitted.

Required bindings cover both physics/command/decision archives, manifests and
public histories, and every consumed primary/auxiliary RGB/depth file, plus
the prospective report and decision stream. All supplied input rosters are
rehashed before and after comparison. The caller still authenticates the
launches, model identities and complete original/new native audits; this
checker does not replace those steps.
