# Prospective family collection: serial/four-process scaling bench V1

One fresh infrastructure-only comparison at
`go2_geometry_progress_family_scaling_v1_attempt_001`, with separate serial and
parallel roots named explicitly in the runner. Both phases execute the same four
fixed training-role specifications: cluster00 left/left-arc, cluster00
right/forward, cluster01 left/hold and cluster01 right/right-arc, appearance
2026090940. The seeded family assignment determines their opaque episode names.
Eight new native episodes total; none may enter the subsequent learning dataset
or geometry-transfer score. No retries, resumption, outcome selection or silent
replacement of failed cases.

Require the complete passing height-union pilot and its exact source/artifact
identities. Retain physical geometry, native gait, command representations,
three quiet history ticks, forty known candidate ticks, 5mm ordered camera,
raw audit and footprint checks. The new family varies the prospective panel
geometry according to its source contract. Every episode runs in a fresh spawned
CPU process. Compare one concurrent process with four, with one OpenCV/BLAS
thread each. Every episode performs full inherited source/input/native
verification before and after execution, and a full raw audit. Include process
startup, verification, recording and audit in measured phase wall time.

Compare every numeric array in every expected NPZ artifact and every decoded RGB
frame by dtype/shape/byte identity, avoiding ZIP timestamp differences. Retain
the complete mismatch list. Require both phases to complete all four raw audits,
no hard measurement failures and full-horizon or contact-stop acquisition.
Select four workers only if those conditions hold, all four signatures agree,
and total speedup is at least1.25. Otherwise select one if the serial phase is
admissible. An inadmissible serial phase selects no collection worker count.
This is an execution choice, not scientific outcome filtering. Infrastructure
failure remains terminal and prevents the subsequent dataset launch.

Freeze bench, collector, episode helper, session, auditor, tensor adapter,
accounting, protocols and focused tests before this bench. The subsequent
collector must use those exact sources; it accepts the terminal bench result
SHA256 explicitly. It may not inherit a verbal claim of benchmark completion.

and storage. Require at least32GiB available RAM,2GiB planned output plus40GiB
