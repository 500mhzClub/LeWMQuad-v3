# Finite RGB-D motion-error members V1 — offline development, no motion

Replay the existing audited shadow-motion neutral/repeated/distinctive recordings,
54 observations per arm, through the actual unchanged ShadowObserver and its
RGBDInertialRayMemory owner. These are separate motion from the failed maze, not
unseen final-test data. The three appearance arms share identical physics and one
layout; do not count them or their perturbations as independent layout trials.
Preserve the original neutral-arm estimator-budget failure and every later
NOT_REINVOKED result. Never resume or reset a failed member.

## Fixed population and inputs

Sixteen members per arm: nominal; positive/negative depth gain .001, depth offset
1 mm, yaw-gyro bias 1 mrad/s, post-anchor specific-force Y bias .01 m/s², initial
velocity mean Y error .01 m/s, independent depth noise amplitude .1 mm; constant
RGB=128; and two combined members with all six numerical errors sharing positive
or negative signs. Not a factorial population or calibrated noise distribution.

Gain and offset affect every valid represented depth before casting back to
float32. No range clipping, validity-mask editing, or discarded fault is allowed.
Independent noise uses a uniform[-1,1] image generated by NumPy default_rng with
SeedSequence([2026090621, decision_ns low32, decision_ns high32]); reuse the pattern
for signs/combined members and matched appearance arms, but regenerate at each
timestamp. Gyro bias affects valid slow and fast samples consistently at every
timestamp including the anchor. Force bias affects only valid Y samples strictly
after the 1.5-s anchor, consistently in overlapping histories. The initial prior
mean changes once; its original radius and provenance remain unchanged. Blank
RGB updates its paired depth packet's RGB identity hash, not its depth or clocks.
Commands, joints, camera geometry, image timing and scene data stay unchanged.

These omit intrinsic/extrinsic uncertainty, joint error, delays, slip distributions,
false matches beyond the chosen appearance conditions, and continuous combinations
of sources. They cannot establish a complete sensor-error set or confidence level.

## Replay, surface query and scoring

Each member has its own causal owner and terminal failure state. Exact nominal
replay must match every original full shadow output before reducing logged fields.
Keep all 54 status rows per member even after termination. Save admitted fusion,
orientation, raw depth motion and point motion; retain rank/status/support changes
and failure chains. No derivative or covariance is computed.

At each member's initial accepted observation, prepare the new bounded surface
from its actual perturbed ranges and estimated up. Fixed surface hypotheses remain
range error .1 mm, tube 1 mm, up error .001; these are conditional surface-model
assumptions, not a claim that they cover every combined member's sensor corruption
relative to the original scene. Record surface rejection rather than search or
change the hypotheses. At observations 0,27,53, if that member remains admitted,
query all actual Go2 primitives at its measured joints and estimated pose in the
initial observed frame. Use zero additional point error for finite-member geometry
isolation only. Keep full footprint coverage and lack of permission explicit.
These short motions may leave the entire underbody unobserved; no virtual forward
configuration substitutes for the actual posture to produce positive coverage.

Persist each arm's predictions before loading its native physics trace for scoring.
Check camera sample indices and timestamps against the existing audited acquisition.
Compute admitted position/orientation errors in the original initial-body frame.
For recorded surface queries, compare estimated versus native body placement
relative to the SAME member's measured reference plane, using exact primitive
support and unchanged joint samples. This is a pose-only reference-gap error, not
an actual-floor calibration or evidence through an unobserved footprint.

Report each member's admitted/terminal/not-reinvoked counts, failure time/reasons,
depth-rank counts, point-complement/fallback counts, point rejection, position and
orientation errors, reference-gap error and observed-footprint counts. Compare
branches only on explicitly shared admitted rows; stopping earlier does not earn
a better full-task score. Retain all failures and do not choose a winning error
amplitude or use sample maximum error as an execution envelope.
