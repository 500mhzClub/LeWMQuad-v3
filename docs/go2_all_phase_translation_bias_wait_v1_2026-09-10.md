# Automatic full-fit to correction handoff

Register one waiter while the original matched-fit waiter2635725 and its
full-fit parent2636352 are alive. Bind both exact launch identities, process
creation times, commands, current boot, and original source union. Require the
original waiter's terminal result and the completed18-model/21600-update result
after both owners end. Recheck every bound artifact and source and reconstruct
the original benchmark concurrency decision. A missing result, failed owner,
changed identity, or48-hour wait expiry preserves the original work and stops
the handoff. Never restart fitting or infer completion from a timeout.

Launch the separately prepared training-only correction runner exactly once,
with the observed completed fit SHA. Its existing full-model admission remains
mandatory before correction, including all18 schedules, ledgers, predictions,
scores and final snapshots. Its existing hardware check requires8GiB available
RAM and256MiB output allowance above the40GiB reserve. This is one CPU correction
process; it performs no native collection and may coexist with the single native
scene. The waiting process creates no training workers. No source or numerical
change to either live fit or prepared correction is permitted.

After correction exits successfully, execute complete correction admission:
reconstruct all30 heads/480 scalars from all4010 training contexts and their
fixed schedules, retaining36 motionless contexts. Save that admission and bind
all outputs, including subprocess output and lifecycle events. Preserve every
failure and partial output. No retry/resume, checkpoint selection, corrected
transfer evaluation, navigation launch or qualification is performed here.
The next native experiment still requires a separately frozen protocol and
current native-idle/resource checks. Output roots are exclusive.
